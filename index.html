Business Case

BrightAssist turns bathroom safety into a scalable smart-home product.

A non-invasive safety device for older adults, caregivers, and senior-living operators, built around automatic lighting, app connectivity, and a roadmap toward privacy-focused sensing.

Market Opportunity

Aging-at-home needs practical safety products, not complicated monitoring systems.

BrightAssist targets families who want safer bathrooms for older adults without cameras, wearables, or expensive smart-home rewiring. The product sits between low-cost night lights and high-cost care monitoring systems.

01

Customer Pain

Bathroom falls are high-risk, but many homes rely on manual light switches, forgotten wearables, or privacy-invasive cameras.

02

Adoption Advantage

Mount-over-switch design reduces installation complexity and makes pilots easier for families, landlords, and care facilities.

03

Expansion Path

The same app and device account model can support multiple rooms, caregiver analytics, and future radar sensing modules.

Risks + Mitigations

The business depends on trust, reliability, and honest safety positioning.

Reliability Risk

Mechanical switch actuation must work repeatedly. Mitigation: durability testing, enclosure fit standards, and simple manual override behavior.

Regulatory + Claims Risk

Fall detection should not be marketed as emergency replacement. Mitigation: position BrightAssist as a safety-assist product and validate radar features before commercial claims.

Installation Variation

Homes use different switch plates and bathroom layouts. Mitigation: modular mounting plates, fit guide, and pilot data.

Customer Acquisition Cost

Consumer safety products need trust. Mitigation: focus on caregiver communities, senior centers, testimonials, and B2B referral channels.

Business Thesis

BrightAssist can become an affordable safety layer for aging-at-home bathrooms.

The near-term opportunity is a low-friction switch kit. The long-term opportunity is recurring caregiver software, multi-room bundles, and privacy-preserving sensing modules.
