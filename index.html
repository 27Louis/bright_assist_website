Why Families Choose It

More confidence at night, less friction during the day.

Bathrooms are one of the highest-risk rooms for older adults. BrightAssist helps by making the first safety step automatic: lights turn on before a person has to search for a switch in the dark. The product is designed for homes, apartments, and assisted-living rooms where privacy and simple installation matter.

01

Automatic Light Control

Motion sensing turns the bathroom light on when someone enters and can turn it off after a quiet period.

02

Privacy First

BrightAssist avoids cameras and microphones, making it better suited for private spaces than video monitoring.

03

Easy Installation

The switch module mounts over a standard light switch and uses a servo to move the switch without changing wall wiring.

04

Family App

Register devices, check status, switch modes, and control lighting from a phone.

How It Works

Safety support in three simple steps.

1

Mount

Place the BrightAssist switch module over the existing bathroom light switch using its fitted enclosure.

2

Connect

Pair the device with the BrightAssist app and choose automatic or manual control.

3

Assist

The sensor detects entry, turns on the light, and syncs state so family members can see the device is active.

Questions

Designed for homes that need help without adding complexity.

Does it require rewiring?

No. The switch module is designed to move an existing switch mechanically.

Does it use a camera?

No. BrightAssist is built around motion and radar sensing, not video.

Is fall detection final?

The radar module is still in development and needs more real-home testing.

Who is it for?

Older adults, caregivers, families, and senior-living pilots.
